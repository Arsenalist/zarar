I've been writing on the web since 2001 and liked it when all my stuff used to be on one site: [arsenalist.com](https://arsenalist.com/). That got split into [raptorsrepublic.com](https://www.raptorsrepublic.com/author/arsenalist/), [depressedprogrammer.wordpress.com](https://depressedprogrammer.wordpress.com/), [arsenalist.wordpress.com](https://arsenalist.wordpress.com/), [bitbytebit.substack.com](https://bitbytebit.substack.com/), [ijtihad.substack.com](https://ijtihad.substack.com/), [dev.to/arsenalist](https://dev.to/arsenalist), java.net, and those don't include the podcasts.

I just turned 44 a few days ago and my birthday resolution was to clean up my online presence so here I am. I tried Obsidian but never really got sync to work as seamlessly so decided to go with Bear Blog because of its sheer simplicity. I've read enough good experiences from people about Bear so I'm quite confident I'm going to like it.

Here goes a reset.
